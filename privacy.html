Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

Overview

Rollscape: Marble Maze ("the App") is developed by Muhammad Haris. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy — the App collects no personal data from its users.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Photos, videos, or contacts
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Browsing or search history

Local Data Storage

All game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults system. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA)
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address (for approximate location-based ad targeting)

On first launch, the App requests your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you decline tracking, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads only.

For more information about how Google handles data, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

The App integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not have access to your Game Center account information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App contains third-party advertising via AdMob.

No Accounts or Servers

The App does not require user accounts, sign-ups, or login. There is no server-side data storage. The App does not communicate with any developer-operated servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
